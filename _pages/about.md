I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Zurich, supervised by Anne Ardila Brenøe and Roberto A. Weber.

My research is in applied microeconomics, with a focus on environmental, labour, gender, and education economics. I combine field experiments, survey experiments, and quasi-experimental methods to study how people make consequential decisions – from how parents' views shape teenagers' occupational choices, to whether sustainability interventions change energy use in the workplace.

I teach Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD level courses at the University of Zurich and co-organise the Econ Industry Talks series.
